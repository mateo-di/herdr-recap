Recap popup, redesigned

Every agent on every machine, in the Herdr app's popup. Colour only where there's state to tell; drawn icons in one family; three weights doing the work the TUI did with Unicode marks. Shown in herdr's Mocha and Latte themes, with made-up agents from Halcyon.

Icons

One family, drawn with the kit's PathBuilder or tinted SVGs: a 16px grid, 1.6 stroke, round ends. State is told by shape as well as colour, so it survives a colour-blind theme.

Motion

  • Working spins its arc (spin), natively, only while drawn.
  • Needs you sends a soft ring out every 1.8s (loop on scale and opacity), so it's found without reading.
  • A card that changes group springs to its new place (spring on position); the selection's edge slides between cards (transition, 140ms).
  • Nothing else moves. Reduced motion stops all of it.

What changes from today

  • Unicode marks (◉ ● ◔ ✓) become drawn icons; PR and Linear states get their real glyphs.
  • The chosen card lifts: raised surface, soft shadow, an edge in its state's colour.
  • A question waiting for you is a callout with its own Reply, not a red line.
  • Details become chips: the branch in mono, tasks as a small bar, the PR and issue in their colours.
  • Group headings become small caps with a rule; tabs a segmented control with state dots.
  • Keys in the footer are keycaps.
